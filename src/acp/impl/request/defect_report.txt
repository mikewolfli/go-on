## Issue A6 [P2] background.rs: Lock-then-release window between with_acp_lock and spawn_blocking

Description: The pattern `with_acp_lock(...|guard|guard.clone())` followed by `spawn_blocking(move || ...)`

blocking operation starts. Another thread could modify or swap the cache/vector reference in between.
